In this roguelike adventure, you play as a Unicorn on a quest to find the missing rainbows. Explore, survive, and collect them all!

Controls:
- Use arrow keys to move.
- Every move consumes 1 Health Point (HP). Plan your route carefully to reach your goal before running out of health.
